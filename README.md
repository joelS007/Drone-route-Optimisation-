# Drone-route-Optimisation-
                                    Optmising drone routes in terms of energy consumption and time

The project's primary goal is to develop a model using a heuristic hybrid algorithm that identifies 
the most efficient route for a drone to transport a payload to specified delivery points while 
considering factors such as energy consumption, flight time, payload speed, and avoiding no-fly 
zones. The end goal is to ensure that the drone can reach the delivery points and return to the 
original warehouse where it was deployed in the most optimised and effective manner. The ob
jectives considered for this project were the minimisation of energy consumption and time 
which was a bi-objective type. The problem was divided into two generating optimal routes using 
GA and NSGA-2 and comparing their performance the second part is building a model with GA 
considering the no-fly zone. These models were successful in achieving their goals with the lim
ited computational resources and time frame. The objectives were treated well in all models and 
comparatively, NSGA-2 performed much better.

THE THREE FILES INCLUDES OF DIFFERENT VERSIONS OF THIS PROJECTS UNDER DIFFETENT ALGORITHMS

mTSP-NSGA-II FOR NON DOMINATED SORTED GENETIC ALGORITHM
